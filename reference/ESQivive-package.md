# ESQivive: IVIVE of ADME Parameters for PBK Models

In vitro to in vivo extrapolation (IVIVE) of absorption, distribution,
metabolism and excretion (ADME) parameters, to derive inputs for
physiologically based kinetic (PBK) models. Predicts the fraction
unbound in microsomal and hepatocyte incubations and in plasma, looks up
measured fraction unbound values, fits substrate depletion and
Michaelis-Menten curves, scales in vitro clearance to in vivo, and
derives intestinal permeability from Caco-2 and effective permeability
values. The functions are developed for use with the Open Systems
Pharmacology Suite ('PK-Sim' and 'MoBi').
